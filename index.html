Setup

Add the source

Choose your package manager. The same URL works in both.

S

Sileo

  1. Open Sileo and go to Sources.
  2. Tap + and paste the repository URL.
  3. Browse the new source and install your tweaks.
Add to Sileo
Z

Zebra

  1. Open Zebra and switch to the Sources tab.
  2. Tap + and paste the repository URL.
  3. Tap Add Source to refresh the catalog.
